Part 1: Data privacy policy

Who we are

MoreGrowth Digital Solutions is a company registered in the Sultanate of Oman and is the controller of data collected through moregrowth.co. Privacy questions: info@moregrowth.co.

What we collect

  • Consultation form: name, email, company, phone (optional), a short note on your goal (optional) and the service you are interested in.
  • Usage data: only with your consent, aggregated data about your visit (pages, device type, approximate country, traffic source) through Google Analytics.
  • Preferences on your device: your light/dark mode choice and your cookie decision are stored in your browser and are not sent to us.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your request, schedule your consultation and follow up on it.
  • To understand and improve how the site is used, only with your consent.
  • To meet legal obligations where required.

We do not sell your data, use it for targeted advertising without consent, or add you to marketing lists unless you ask us to.

Cookies and consent

We use Google Consent Mode v2. Analytics stays off until you click "Accept" in the cookie banner; if you decline, no analytics cookies are stored. You can change your choice at any time with the button at the top of this page.

Who processes data for us

  • Google Tag Manager & Google Analytics: traffic measurement, after consent.
  • Web3Forms: delivers consultation-form messages to our inbox.
  • Google Workspace: hosts our email.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.

These providers may process data outside your country; we choose providers that apply internationally recognised safeguards.

How long we keep it

We keep consultation requests and correspondence as long as needed to serve you, and delete them within 24 months of our last contact unless the law requires longer. Google Analytics data is retained for 14 months.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to its processing, or withdraw consent at any time. If you are in the EU you also have the right to data portability and to complain to your data protection authority. Email info@moregrowth.co and we will reply within 30 days.

Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), access to data is limited to team members who need it, and our accounts use two-factor authentication.

Children

Our services are for organisations and businesses. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Part 2: Responsible use of AI

Our commitment

We build AI solutions that serve our clients' business goals and respect the rights of their staff and customers. These principles govern every project, from assessment to operation.

People stay in charge

AI suggests and speeds things up; consequential decisions stay with your team. Every solution we design has clear human review and approval points.

Client data privacy

  • We use client data only for the agreed purpose and only as much as needed.
  • We use enterprise tiers of AI platforms that do not train their models on your data.
  • We never put sensitive personal data into an AI system without written consent and appropriate safeguards.
  • We sign confidentiality and data-processing agreements where needed.

Transparency

We tell clients where and how AI is used in a solution and what its accuracy limits are, and we recommend telling end users when they are talking to an automated assistant.

Accuracy and fairness

We test solutions on realistic data, including Arabic, before launch, review outputs for errors and bias, and keep monitoring and improving performance after go-live.

Security and accountability

We apply least-privilege access, log important operations and name a clear owner for every system. If a material error happens, we act quickly and inform the client.

Legal compliance

We follow applicable data protection laws, including Oman's Personal Data Protection Law and the EU GDPR for clients in Europe, and track the requirements of the EU AI Act where it applies.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time; the date at the top shows the latest version. Questions? Email info@moregrowth.co.
