Our story

From a growing team to an office under rubble

01

What we built

In Gaza we built MoreGrowth Digital Solutions: a team of more than 15 people whose salaries supported many families. Since 2020 we have been a Google Partner, serving 15 clients in Saudi Arabia, Palestine, Kuwait and Bahrain with digital marketing, paid ads, content and marketing consulting.

02

What happened

During the war our office was destroyed three times. Each time we gathered what was left and started again, until there was nothing left to gather. Contracts stopped, projects were lost, and the team was scattered.

03

Why we didn't stop

The company is still standing, with team members working today in Oman and Palestine, but without an office. We have rebuilt our website and brand and are back in touch with clients. What we lack is the one thing determination alone can't build: six months of running costs until the business can carry itself again.
